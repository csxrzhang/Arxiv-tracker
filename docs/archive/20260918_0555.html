Objective vs. Search: Decomposing What Makes a Good Tokeniser

Authors: Ahmetcan Yavuz, Clara Meister, Tiago Pimentel
Venue: EMNLP 2026
类别：cs.CL · cs.AI，链接：Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2 · Code3

The parity gap in crystal tensor prediction
标题：晶体张量预测中的宇称差距
Authors: Can Polat, Mustafa Kurban, Erchin Serpedin
类别：cs.LG，链接：Abs · PDF · Code1 · Code2
